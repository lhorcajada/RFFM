## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Minimum squad structure
After applying the proposal, the players that remain called SHALL include, whenever the available squad allows it: at least 2 goalkeepers, 3 full-backs and wing-backs, 3 centre-backs, 4 central midfielders (defensive and offensive), 2 wide players and 1 forward. Only available players (squad minus those already deconvoked) SHALL count. Players SHALL count towards a minimum through their active position. A player SHALL also count through a possible (non-active) demarcation, only if their own role stays at or above its minimum without them. That compensation SHALL be allowed for mandatory and non-mandatory deconvocations alike. There SHALL be no extra cover minimum for wide players or forwards.

#### Scenario: Three full-backs not deconvoked together
- **WHEN** 4 full-backs are available, one is already deconvoked and the target asks for 2 more
- **THEN** at most 1 more full-back is proposed so that 3 remain

#### Scenario: Compensation by a versatile player
- **WHEN** two centre-backs are candidates, 3 centre-backs would remain required, and a full-back with Central among their possible demarcations belongs to a line of 4 full-backs
- **THEN** both centre-backs can be proposed and the full-back counts as centre-back cover

#### Scenario: Versatile player whose line would break
- **WHEN** the only versatile player belongs to a line that is at its minimum
- **THEN** that player does not count as cover and the centre-back is protected

#### Scenario: Players already out do not count
- **WHEN** 4 centre-backs are in the squad and 2 are already deconvoked by technical decision
- **THEN** only 2 centre-backs count as available and the minimum is reported as unmet «Centrales: quedan 2 de 3»
