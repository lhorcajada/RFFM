## Why

The page action bar (the strip of buttons at the top of each screen: save, back, export) used to scroll away with the content, so on long screens those buttons were out of reach. The bar now stays pinned directly below the app header so the page actions are always visible.

## What Changes

- `ActionBar.module.css` adds a shared `.sticky` class (`position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 5`, below the header's 1600) that `.actionBar` composes, so the bar pins inside the existing `BaseLayout` scroll area without changing `BaseLayout`.
- The action bar background changes from a translucent blurred fill to the opaque `--rffm-card-bg` CSS custom property, so content passing underneath does not show through.
- `ActionBar.tsx` measures its real height with `ResizeObserver` and publishes it as `--rffm-action-bar-h` on its nearest vertically scrolling ancestor, updating it on resize and removing it on unmount.
- The top bars of `NewSessionPage`, `NewExercisePage` and `ContentBoardPage` compose the shared `sticky` class; the `ContentBoardPage` top bar gets the `--rffm-coach-bg` background.
- The pinned panels of `GameModelReadingView` and `Classification` add `--rffm-action-bar-h` to their sticky `top`; the `GameModelReadingView` panel also subtracts it from its `max-height`.
- New Vitest tests cover the published height (mount, resize, unmount), `ContentLayout` with and without `actionBar`, and a portal mounted inside the bar.
- `Front/package.json` version bumps from 1.151.0 to 1.152.0.

The page title (`PageHeader`) keeps scrolling, and section bars inside tabs or blocks (including `DashboardActionBar`) are not pinned; the trade-off accepted is that a bar that wraps on a narrow screen takes more of the visible area while pinned, and that page bars and section bars now behave differently.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `page-action-bar`: the page action bar and the three own page-top bars stay pinned below the app header with an opaque background, publish their height, and pinned panels offset themselves below it.

### Modified Capabilities

- None
